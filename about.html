०२/ 02 · About

Paper, pigment & a bad mood.

Practice
Collage · poster · photography
Archive
29 plates, 2023 — 2026
Tools
Phone, Picsart
Based
India · --:-- IST

Most of this starts as an ordinary photo on a phone. Then I cut into it, re-colour it, and give it a line it didn't ask for.

The archive is twenty-nine plates made between December 2023 and October 2026. Some are collages built from whatever was nearby: family-function costumes, temple jewellery, zari borders, comic bursts, a yellow dialog box from an old desktop. Some are posters, where a title in Latin or Devanagari does as much work as the picture. The rest are photographs, mostly at night, pushed until the light starts to smear.

The references are close to home: arthouse film posters, truck-art lettering, block-print textiles, brutalist courtyards in hard afternoon sun, street-punk zines that say the rude thing out loud. Nothing here is meant to look polite.

  1. Paper over pixelsEvery plate is presented like a mounted print: a mat, a border, a stamp. Screens are flat; the work shouldn't feel it.
  2. Two pigments, used surgicallySindoor and haldi do the shouting. Everything else stays quiet so they can.
  3. Say it in two scriptsLatin for the reading, Devanagari for the second voice: an index, a date, a joke.
  4. Keep the attitudeIf a dancer hates her costume, the caption says so.
