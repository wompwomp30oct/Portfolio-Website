०४/ 04

About the practice

Who makes these and why.

I like pictures that talk back. A dancer who hates her costume. A dog with opinions. A bus that burned and kept its date.

This archive collects twenty-nine plates made between December 2023 and October 2026: collage, poster type, and photographs taken after dark. Nearly all of it was cut, graded and lettered on a phone, using whatever was nearby: album photos, temple jewellery, textile borders, signboards, comic bursts.

The site is set like the work: warm paper, deep courtyard shadow, two pigments, and Devanagari wherever a number needs a second voice.
